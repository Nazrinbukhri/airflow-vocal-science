# 48-hour training curriculum

The programme is defined in `src/lib/curriculum/programme.ts` (tested in `programme.test.ts`).

- **1 "hour" = 1 session**: 45 minutes of study + 15 minutes of rest.
- **Not 48 hours in a row.** Suggested maximum: 3 sessions per day. Day 1 / Day 2 are the two halves of the programme.
- **Resumable**: progress is saved on the device; the dashboard always shows the next unfinished session.

## Day 1 – Foundation and attacking principles

| Hours | Block | Sessions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Chessboard and pieces | What is chess / 64 squares · Files and ranks · The six pieces |
| 4–6 | Rules, legal moves, notation | Movement & capture · Castling, en passant, promotion · SAN and UCI |
| 7–9 | Check, checkmate, mating patterns | Escaping check · Checkmate, stalemate, draws · Basic mates |
| 10–12 | Centre control and development | Why the centre · Develop minor pieces · King safety & castling |
| 13–15 | Italian Game | 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 · Pressure on f7 · Attacking plans |
| 16–18 | Basic tactics | Hanging pieces · Checks and threats · Double attack |
| 19–21 | Pawn structures and formations | Chains, isolated, doubled · Open files, outposts · Passed pawns, fianchetto |
| 22–24 | First assessment and practice | Day-1 test · Practice game vs AI · Review own game |

## Day 2 – Attacking mastery

| Hours | Block | Sessions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25–28 | Aggressive openings | Sicilian ×2 · King's Indian ×2 |
| 29–32 | Fork, pin, skewer, discovered attack | Knight fork · Pin & skewer · Discovered attack · Deflection/decoy |
| 33–35 | King-side attacks and sacrifices | Greek gift · Smothered/Arabian/Anastasia · King hunt |
| 36–38 | Defence and counterattack | Opponent's threats · Defending accurately · Zwischenzug |
| 39–41 | Endgame fundamentals | Active king & passed pawns · Opposition · Rook mate |
| 42–44 | Stockfish analysis and prediction | Reading evaluations · Candidate moves · Calculate two replies |
| 45–46 | Grandmaster attacking games | Tal & Kasparov · Polgár, Fischer, Carlsen, Nakamura |
| 47–48 | Final challenge and report | Final challenge · Performance report |

## Teaching method

Every lesson follows **EXPLAIN → SHOW → PRACTICE → TEST → REVIEW**
(*Terang → Tunjuk → Latih → Uji → Ulang kaji*). Hints are progressive:
threat → attacked pieces → candidate squares → solution with explanation.
